## Amendment 136 (REGISTERED 2026-11-26): the kill channel with healing; the total has no upper limit (joint with EnginePerf; no run)
Ruled by Caden (Discord 2026-11-26 18:56 UTC, relayed by the Overseer): "Heal like stalker unless it's hard to
implement". A creature the player wounded heals in X-Ray at X-Ray's own rates, and the ITR2 puppet follows the healed
health. This amendment changes one line of amendment 134's format and two rules of the ITR2 side. It registers no run.
EnginePerf holds the same text and matches it byte for byte. Everything of amendment 134 not named here stands.

**Why almost nothing changes.** The total is a count of damage dealt, X-Ray applies each new piece once
(`total - applied`), X-Ray's health then regenerates by its own rules, and the puppet shows the snapshot's health
minus `total - applied`. So healing already passes through. Two things were in its way, both on the ITR2 side.

### 1. The pose file (replaces two lines of amendment 134, section 1)
- `<total>`: all damage ITR2 has dealt to this creature in this ITR2 session, as a share of full health, 3 decimals,
  0.000 upward with no upper limit. It never falls within a session.
- A creature that heals can take more than its full health in sum and live (a hit of 0.600, healing, a hit of 0.500:
  total 1.100, `dead` 0). A total of 1.000 or more says nothing about death; only `dead` = 1 does.
- The version stays 4. No field is added or removed. The snapshot file is unchanged.

### 2. ITR2's rules (replace the matching rules of amendment 134, section 3)
- The total is the plain sum of the damage read; the cap at 1.000 is removed, for a hit and for a death alike.
- Shown health is still full health times (the snapshot's health minus `total - applied`), never below 1 %. It is
  written DOWN at once (within one 50 ms pass). It is written UP at most once a second per puppet, to the exact wanted
  value. So while X-Ray heals a creature its puppet may stand below the wanted value by at most one second of X-Ray's
  healing (about 0.01 of full health for a stalker, measured in EnginePerf's kw1).
- Damage is still measured from the value the mod last wrote or read on that puppet, so the share of a hit on a puppet
  standing a little under the wanted value is right.
- Unchanged: the death rule, the 10 s rule, the 60 s rule, the reopened row seeded from X-Ray's applied count.

### 3. X-Ray's rules (EnginePerf's; unchanged, restated because they now matter)
- A total above 1.000 on a live creature is applied like any other: online, the difference to `applied` is applied,
  then `applied = total`; offline it is owed, as in amendment 134. It is not refused and not capped.
- Only a single DIFFERENCE above 1.0 is cut to 1.0 and counted (`kclamped`).
- No ceiling on X-Ray's health: it regenerates by X-Ray's rules. The ceiling proposed earlier today is withdrawn.

### 4. What is built for it (ITR2 side), all behind levers that default to off
Mod blob ad3ca78d0a0a678a20dc3d4beeca403c6735212e (`mod/ITRZone/Scripts/main.lua`).
- Under `t2v=4`: the two caps removed; the upward write limited to one a second per puppet.
- Rig lever for the joint run, `kcj=<s of play clock>` (default 0 = off): at that time the nearest stalker (ITR2 class
  Mimic*) and the nearest other creature are picked among live puppets whose full health was read and whose snapshot
  health is at least 0.9. The pick is tried every pass for 10 s and logged (`KC joint pick stalker|monster id= sec=
  cls= hp= dist_cm= clk=`, or `KC joint pick FAILED`). The stalker takes a hit of `kcjs` (default 0.3) of full health,
  the other creature 0.3. Counted from `kcj`: after `kcjb` s (40) the stalker takes `kcjt` (0.2), after `kcjc` s (60)
  the stalker is killed, after `kcjd` s (85) the other creature is killed. Fixed times. The rig deals engine damage
  and does not tell the mod's bookkeeping, as `kcrig` does (one shared routine).
- Known consequence, stated for the joint run: the rig's hit is a share of FULL health dealt to the puppet's CURRENT
  health. If X-Ray has not healed the creature enough, a second hit can take the puppet to 0, and that is then a death
  by the registered rule (reported with `dead` = 1), not a part hit.

### 5. Offline tests (all PASS on this tree)
- `tools/kc_selftest.sh`: the mod's test with 42 mutants. New: case 18 (the joint rig: pick, shares, order, times, a
  failed pick; 8 mutants), case 19 (healing: 0.6, X-Ray heals 0.4 over 2 s, 0.5 more gives total 1.100 on a live
  creature, the death's total 1.400; upward writes 2 or 3 in 2 s; 4 mutants: each cap put back, upward writes
  unlimited, upward writes off). Case 2 now wants the plain sum 1.250 where it wanted 1.000.
- `tools/kc1_selftest.py` (kc1's scorer on the offline loop) and `tools/kc_feed_selftest.py`: PASS unchanged.
- With every lever off the existing offline tests pass and the arm C, arm D and finer-clock witness logs are
  byte-identical to master's (c415ffc).
- EnginePerf's side: an offline check with a mutant that a total of 1.100 on a live creature with 0.600 applied is
  applied as 0.500, not refused, not capped, not counted in `kclamped`.

### 6. Limits
- Not shown in a game yet: that a puppet's health written upward about once a second under ITR2's AI disturbs nothing
  (kc1 showed single writes are kept exactly and the AI goes on). The joint run is the first to show it.
- X-Ray's healing rates are X-Ray's; ITR2's own classes do not heal by themselves (none seen in kc1's 215 s).
- kc1 (amendment 135) was run with the cap in place; none of its events reached a total above 1.000, so its result
  stands for everything but the cap.
