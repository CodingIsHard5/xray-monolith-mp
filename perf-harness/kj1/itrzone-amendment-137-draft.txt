outcome by name). Story characters stand at the track's start (EnginePerf's boot dump has one about 27 m off, beside four ordinary
stalkers); the pick now leaves them out, so S is one of the ordinary ones or the run is a NO VERDICT. If X-Ray's
kill call does not kill S, their `dead` line is still written, `snap=` shows it alive and J5 fails. ITR2's AI or another puppet hurts S or M (J7). A health write every second disturbs a puppet
under ITR2's AI (not scored directly; a vanished or stuck puppet would show in J4 to J7 and in the reported lines).
X-Ray is slow after its stop (J6, and their checks 9 and 10). No healthy monster near the track's start, or a generic
stalker listed but without a synced puppet (J1).
