# R1 close-out: render feed off the main thread (STOPPED, 2026-11-22)

Ruling (Overseer, ruling A, after aabfbe198): R1 STOPS by its registered abort. main = 0.587 ms in Rostok against
the 0.6 ms bar. A near miss still counts as a miss. Rostok passed every sanity check. The Cordon frame-sanity
failure (+7.9 % against the fresh K0 runs) lies outside the render feed and inside K0's own spread (4.10-4.52 ms).
It is recorded as unexplained Cordon variance, and there is no retry. Cordon's zone numbers are not used for anything.
(An earlier Overseer message called it probe perturbation; the later ruling's wording stands.)

## What we learned
- Step 0: the main thread's render feed is 2.75 ms per frame in Rostok and 1.83 ms in Cordon. Almost all of it is
  submission (b): 2.13 and 1.64 ms. Building the render lists (a) is small: 0.54 and 0.14 ms.
- Step 1a: the main scene passes (static0 + dynamic0 + forward) are only 0.587 ms of Rostok's 2.13 ms of submission.
  The largest of them, static0, is 0.395 ms.
- The rest of submission is the sun cascades, 1.13 ms in Rostok and 0.48 ms in Cordon, plus many other passes,
  each small: lights, shadows, HUD, details, sky, accumulation. The cost is spread thin.
- Deferred contexts would need a large refactor (the global RCache, shared constant buffers, per-frame globals). The
  one-pass slice would recover at most part of 0.4-0.6 ms, about 10 % of the frame, before overheads. Not worth it.
- Probe overhead matters at this scale: about 80k probe calls per frame, at about 9 ns each, is about 0.7 ms. The
  hot probes are now compiled out under /DXRCORE_PROFILER_COARSE (PROF_EVENT_HOT).
- My predictions were wrong twice: DROP at step 0 (the rule picked SUBMISSION), and main 0.65 ms at 1a.

## What would revive it
- The sun cascades: the biggest single block of submission in Rostok (1.13 ms). R_sun.cpp is gammavr-bd's file, so
  this waits for their 8c runs to end. Any change there needs their conditions: they see the diff first, it lives on
  a perf/* branch for them to cherry-pick, the cascade order and per-eye state stay as they are, and nothing is cached
  per frame number.
- A new measurement showing a bigger main-scene share: a denser scene, more players in view, or VR's two eyes.
- Native D3D11 numbers: the DXVK results do not carry over unmeasured.
